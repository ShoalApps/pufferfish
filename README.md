# Swordfish Music

A YouTube Music player in Material 3 Expressive: search songs, albums and artists, background playback with a media notification, a radio that keeps going after your queue, synced lyrics, and a now-playing screen coloured from the album art.

Part of [Swordfish](https://github.com/SwordfishApps). GPL-3.0. Not affiliated with YouTube or Google.
